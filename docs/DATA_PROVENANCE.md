Open questions before ticking:
- **CallCenterEN is non-commercial**, the reason Qwen2.5-3B was rejected. It is
  meant to shape the ASR noise (confidence and disfluency statistics), not to
  enter the corpus as text; decide whether that use is acceptable, or drop it.
- **CIEMPIESS Light is share-alike.** Only its audio's error profile (via
  Scribe v2) is used; decide whether anything derived from it is redistributed.

### Replay candidates (H5b), surveyed 2026-10-03 — Aya chosen

The gold set needs `gold.replay_ratio` x `gold.target_size` = 1,200 replay rows.
Human-written data only: translated English sets add translationese, and sets
generated with GPT models carry OpenAI's terms on training competing models.

## Carrier-phrase bank

`data/carrier_phrases/v1.jsonl` is generated by Qwen/Qwen3-8B (Apache-2.0)
with `prompts/carrier_phrases/v1.yaml`, filtered by code, and approved phrase
by phrase by a native speaker (`python -m src.pipeline.synth.carrier review`).
Every entry records its prompt hash, seed, reviewer and review time; only
approved entries reach the corpus. The review cannot be regenerated: version
the bank with DVC and keep a copy off this disk.

**Rejected (license):**
- Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct — Qwen Research License, non-commercial.
- CALLHOME Spanish — LDC license, paid and not redistributable.

**Dropped (scope, not license):**
- SGD, Taskmaster, MultiWOZ — hotel and restaurant domains; they would need
  translation and do not teach alphanumeric capture.
- TEDx Spanish — the ASR error profile uses CIEMPIESS Light plus own recordings.
- Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Apache-2.0, verified) — replaced by Qwen3-1.7B so H5a
  compares sizes within one model family.

## Gemini API (free tier)

Gemini is used only as the H1/H1b frontier arm and as the evaluation judge. No
Gemini output enters the training corpus, so the clause against using the
service "to develop models that compete with the Services" does not apply. On
the free tier Google may use submitted content to improve its products: only the
frozen eval set is sent (it holds no personal data) and the paper discloses it,
including the side effect that later Gemini versions may have seen the eval set.

## Synthetic data

Dialogues generated by this pipeline are recorded with: generating model and version,
prompt version, generation parameters, seed, and date. Template-generated examples
record the carrier-phrase bank version instead of a model; the bank itself records
its generator (model id, e.g. Qwen/Qwen3-8B) and its human reviewer. Declared as synthetic in the
data card. Never presented as real conversations.
