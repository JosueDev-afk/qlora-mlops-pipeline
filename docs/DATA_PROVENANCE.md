## Gemini API terms

The Additional Terms state: "You may not use the Services to develop models that
compete with the Services." The project's position is that a narrow es-MX
extractor trained partly on Gemini-written carrier phrases does not compete with
Gemini. **This interpretation is the maintainer's call and must be recorded here
before the bank is generated.** Use the paid tier only: on the unpaid tier Google
uses submitted content to improve its products. The fallback with no ambiguity is
to write the bank with Qwen3.

## Synthetic data

Dialogues generated by this pipeline are recorded with: generating model and version,
prompt version, generation parameters, seed, and date. Template-generated examples
record the carrier-phrase bank version instead of a model; the bank itself records
its generator (Gemini model id or Qwen3) and its human reviewer. Declared as synthetic in the
data card. Never presented as real conversations.
